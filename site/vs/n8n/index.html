  • theloop vs n8n
  • 2-minute read

n8n builds automations.
theloop powers products.

n8n lets you drag boxes onto a canvas to automate work, and its AI Agent node puts a model in the middle. theloop has no canvas at all. It's the engine you plug into your own product, so your customers get an assistant.

In plain words.

n8n

A Lego table.

Snap triggers, apps and an AI agent together on a canvas, then watch it run. Ideal for automating your own team's work, no code needed.

theloop

A motor in a sealed box.

No table, no bricks, no screen. You bolt it into your product and drive it with HTTP calls.

Side by side.

Which one is yours?

Pick n8n if…

  • You're automating your own company's processes
  • People who don't code should be able to build and change it
  • You need lots of app integrations fast
  • You want tool approvals in Slack or Telegram out of the box

Pick theloop if…

  • The assistant is part of a product you sell
  • You need a permissive license with no strings
  • Your product already has a backend or a low-code platform to drive it
  • You bill customers per run

They can meet. An n8n workflow behind a webhook is just an HTTPS endpoint, so it can serve as a tool for theloop.

Less canvas.
More product.
